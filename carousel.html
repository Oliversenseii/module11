Carousel

Definition

A carousel, also called a slider, is a component that shows a set of images or cards one at a time. Users move between items using buttons, dots, or swipe gestures, which lets a lot of content fit in a small space.

When to use
  • You have a lot of content and little space
  • The items are visual, like photos or cards
  • The content is nice to see but not essential
When to avoid
  • Important information everyone must see
  • Long text that needs careful reading
  • Auto-play that users cannot pause
Portfolio tip

Can this be used in a portfolio?

Yes. A carousel is a good way to showcase your projects. Give each slide a screenshot, a title, a short description, and a link. Keep it to three to five slides so visitors see your best work first.
